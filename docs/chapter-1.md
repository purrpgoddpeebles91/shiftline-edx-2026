# Chapter 1 Prototype

## Chapter title
The Fractured Gate

## Summary
The player reaches the first major breach in reality: a collapsed gate between the living city and the Rift. This chapter teaches the core mechanics: movement, dodging, shifting, combat, and survival under unstable conditions.

## Structure

### Segment A: Ruin approach
- Side-scrolling traversal through broken city streets
- Small enemy groups attack in shallow waves
- Shards are visible as collectible pickups
- Short narrative text introduces the Shift thesis

### Segment B: Gate line
- Narrow platforming path with hazard distortion
- Shift state affects broken platforms and enemy patterns
- Checkpoint system begins

### Segment C: Rift boss
- A first boss appears at the gate
- The boss attacks in phase-based patterns
- The player must shift at the correct moment to survive and counterattack
- Defeat triggers chapter completion and upgrade screen

## Boss concept
### Name
The Warden of the Seams

### Role
A giant sentinel made from fractured reality and ancient metal, stationed at the first breach.

### Phase behavior
- Phase 1: slow long-range shots, simple platforming pressure
- Phase 2: adds sweeping curtain shots and faster tracking projectiles
- Phase 3: the boss tears open the Rift and deploys environment spikes

### Shift interaction
- Normal state: easiest to attack
- Echo state: best for survival and repositioning
- Rift state: strongest damage burst but increased exposure

## Win condition
The player survives all phases and shatters the gate while preserving their own state through the collapse.

## Chapter reward
A set of upgrades and a narrative log fragment revealing the first truths about the Shift.
